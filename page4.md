# Eiriaoloth

My book is *Eiriaoloth*, an epic fantasy through the beautiful and lush land, soon to be ruined by the rising flood and the central conquest of the Sword. The Destroyer is coming, ready to clasp Elvonar in his claws. Who can stop him now?

Certainly not the meek inhabitants of Fornitas, or even the more powerful Guardians. The doom marches on, unchecked. But the Redeemer is still there, and Elvonar shall not fall while He still breaths. The question is, how long will that be?

Cast of characters;

Aithon––main character. A seemingly unimportant villager from Fornitas. He feels lost and forsaken.

Asinia––A fierce, loyal, Guardian, who has the ability to shift between human and dragon. She and Kironi do not get along together very well.

Eirian––A quiet, gentle healer who knows Kironi well and will help those in need.

Kironi––Kironi is loyal, quick with his knives, devoted to Eirian, and constantly in over his head.

Melias––A strange elusive wizard who is ready to guide and protect those who need it.

Tharias––An experienced warrior with more than standard sword-fighting up his sleeves...

Narsendor––An evil ship captain who kidnaps Eirian.

Zerinsray––Evil sorcerer, king of the Iron Realm.

Zarana––His equally evil wife.

The Dragon Master––A messenger from the Three who guides those correctly on the path to becoming Guardians.

The Sword––A mysterious weapon lurking at the bottom of the sea surrounding Elvonar. It has maybe something to do with the tsunami of the Dark Tide.

The Destoyer––The master demon, who lives to tempt and corrupt.

The Three––The Trinity, who have guarded the world since They created it.

(End of cast.)
